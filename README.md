# **Inventory Management Dashboard (Power BI Project)**

This project focuses on developing an **Inventory Management Dashboard** using **Power BI**, designed to help **Warehouse and In-Plant Inventory Managers** effectively control stock levels, maintain service levels, and improve overall inventory efficiency.

---

## **📘 Project Overview**
The dashboard provides real-time visibility into stock movements and warehouse performance.  
It applies **ABC and XYZ classifications**, calculates **inventory turnover ratios**, and helps identify **reorder points** to optimize inventory control and minimize carrying costs.

---

## **🧰 Tools Used**
- **Power BI**
- **Excel**

---

## **📊 Key Components**
- **ABC Classification** – Categorization based on product value and contribution.  
- **XYZ Classification** – Categorization based on demand variability.  
- **Inventory Turnover Ratio** – Measures how often inventory is sold and replaced.  
- **Safety Stock Levels** – Helps ensure sufficient stock to meet service levels.  
- **Reorder Level & Safety Stock Estimation** – Identifies when to replenish items.  
- **Average Weekly Demand Calculation** – Tracks demand trends over time.  
- **Stock Status & Forecasting** – Predicts inventory requirements for upcoming periods.

---

## **📈 Visualizations Used**
- **Stacked Area Chart** – Displays average and cumulative performance of ABC categories.  
- **Cards, Gauges, and Slicers** – Show summary metrics and area-based filtering.  
- **Tables, Matrix, and Line Charts** – Represent stock reports, distribution, and turnover ratios.  
- **DAX Functions** – Used for custom calculations and time intelligence analysis.  
- **Calculated Columns & Measures** – Created using DAX for combining dates and dynamic metrics.

---

## **⚙️ Steps Followed**
1. **In Power BI → Get Data → Upload Excel File**  
2. **Perform ETL (Extract, Transform, Load)** operations for data cleaning and preparation.  
3. **Apply DAX operations** for calculated measures and KPIs.  
4. **Build interactive visualizations** using charts, cards, and slicers.  
5. **Format and design** the dashboard for user-friendly reporting.
